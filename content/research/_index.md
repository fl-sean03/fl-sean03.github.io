title: "Research"
---

Deep technical investigation into engineering problems that matter. Distinct from [writings](/writings/) (essays and arguments) and [projects](/projects/) (built artifacts), this section hosts research corpora, which are sustained, multi-document investigations of specific technical domains.

The first corpus, [Matter Compilation](/research/matter-compilation/) (March 2026), is 13 documents on building arbitrary physical structures with atomic precision. The second, [Territorial Engineering](/research/territorial-engineering/) (April 2026), is 16 essays on when building new coastline is a sensible response to a real constraint. It was rewritten in July 2026, and the [original edition](/research/territorial-engineering/original/) is still available.
