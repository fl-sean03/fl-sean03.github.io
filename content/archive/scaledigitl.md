Every mid-market company is being told they need to "adopt AI." Most adoption projects are scoped as multi-quarter transformations led by consultancies that bill by the hour and deliver slides. The actual AI-enabled workflow is two engineers and a weekend of prompt engineering.

The thesis is to productize the prompt-engineering weekend with fixed-scope engagements. Pick a workflow, wire it up, ship it in two weeks. No slides, no steering committees, no discovery phase. Price it like a SaaS onboarding fee.

I built an agency landing page at scaledigitl.com with hero, positioning, and CTA.

It's parked because consulting is a sales-heavy business and I hate sales. The thesis is right. There's money in the gap between "need AI" and "hire Accenture." Someone with a network and a patience for enterprise procurement should run it.
