# Attribution and reuse

The slide text, notes and pictures were written or drawn for this presentation. No figure, image or wording from a private lecture deck or a third-party paper is reproduced. Published papers establish the scientific facts and reported measurements. Slide footers give short source names, and the reading version, the presenter notes and `citation-license-manifest.json` give the full references, including those cited only in the notes.

The crystal structures come from the Crystallography Open Database, whose entries are dedicated to the public domain under CC0, or from published lattice sizes. Molecules were built with RDKit and surfaces with ASE, and the water electron cloud was computed with PySCF. Each is cited where the deck first uses it and listed in `citation-license-manifest.json`. The Lennard-Jones curves, forces and worked numbers are calculations from the cited published parameters. Pictures of molecules on surfaces are illustrations, not simulation snapshots, and published results were not rerun.

Lato is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Its full copyright and licence notices are in `source/fonts/NOTICES.txt` and embedded in the viewer, and the font files keep their own licence.

Facts and parameter values are attributed to the primary papers listed in the manifest. No further reuse licence is granted for the presentation’s own text or artwork. Third-party papers and private lecture files are not included in this bundle.
