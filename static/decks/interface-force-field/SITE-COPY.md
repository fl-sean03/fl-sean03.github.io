# Interface Force Field: From atoms to evidence

I use this presentation to explain how atomic structures, chemical assignments, model parameters and experimental references connect to testable material predictions. I follow source-grounded rhodium structures, published lattice and surface calibration, and mechanical predictions that were not fitted to those targets. I also explain the IFF Agent workflow and the complementary roles of classical models, DFT and learned interatomic potentials.

Explore the presentation, download the editable PowerPoint or static PDF, and view the structure, energy/force and evidence animations. The motion is illustrative, and I have not rerun the published results for this presentation.
